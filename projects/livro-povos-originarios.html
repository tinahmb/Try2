Livro: Povos Originários do Brasil

As part of my coursework at Labjor, I collaborated with two classmates on a book chapter examining the absence of Indigenous peoples within the Sustainable Development Goals and the 2030 Agenda, in the Brazilian context. My contribution focused on bridging the human rights perspective with the country's contextual realities, developing two sections: "A Agenda 2030 e a causa indígena" and "Paradigma dos direitos humanos."
