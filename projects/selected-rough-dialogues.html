Selected Rough Dialogues

Selected Rough Dialogues is a weekly newsletter for anyone drawn to the diffuse, often complex conversations at the intersection of human rights, civic tech, and digital governance. I write from my own perspective, reflecting on the cultural nuances that emerge where technology meets the public interest, unpacking policy shifts and exploring ethical tensions in design and infrastructure.
