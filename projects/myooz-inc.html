MYOOZ InC

MYOOZ InC is an independent record label. We produce and distribute music for a roster of artists across multiple countries. Take a look at our site to hear what we’ve been working on and get to know the roster.
As a personal and experimental project I also built a stem separation tool to split tracks into individual components for remixing, sampling, and production work.
