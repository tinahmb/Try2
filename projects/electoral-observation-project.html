Electoral Observation Project

In 2018, while at the Fray Francisco de Vitoria Human Rights Center, I served as operational coordinator for a national observation project during Mexico's presidential election, driven by the Red Universitaria y Ciudadana por la Democracia (RUCD), a Mexican civil society network working in defense of democracy.
We received UNDP funding and managed communications and security monitoring for 303 observers deployed across 7 states, coordinating a distributed network of 170+ institutional partners from 77 universities and 12 civil society organizations spanning 13 countries. We also organized certification workshops and oversaw logistics for 100+ international observers.
(Part of this experience and its results were captured in the RUCD's official 2018 report.)
(Our work was also picked up by outlets like Forbes México, Aristegui Noticias, La Jornada, and The Nation.)
