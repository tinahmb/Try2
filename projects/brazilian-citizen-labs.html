Brazilian Citizen Labs

For my master's thesis I focused on citizen innovation laboratories and other technocultural initiatives in Brazil, mapping organizations across platforms like CIVICS, Fablabs.io, Garoa Hacker Clube, and Hackerspaces.org.
The research explores how these spaces: Citizen Labs, Fab Labs, and Hackerspaces, foster collaborative participation in digital design and contribute to the democratization of technology, looking closely at their organizational dynamics, funding models, and relationships with public policy and community.
I also wrote about this mapping exercise, and the data gathered, on my Substack newsletter.
