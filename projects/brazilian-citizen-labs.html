Brazilian Citizen Labs

Project overview

For my master's thesis I focused on citizen innovation laboratories and other technocultural initiatives in Brazil, mapping organizations across platforms like CIVICS, Fablabs.io, Garoa Hacker Clube, and Hackerspaces.org.

The research explored how these spaces — Citizen Labs, Fab Labs, and Hackerspaces — foster collaborative participation in digital design and contribute to the democratization of technology, looking closely at their organizational dynamics, funding models, and relationships with public policy and community.

I also created a few data visualizations and wrote more about this mapping exercise, and the data gathered, on my Substack newsletter.
